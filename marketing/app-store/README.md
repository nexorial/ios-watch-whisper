# App Store metadata

Micodex 1.0 is a free Watch-only release with a separate Mac receiver installed through Homebrew or its notarized GitHub release.

- Primary category: Productivity; secondary: Developer Tools.
- Localizations: English (U.S.) and Simplified Chinese. English listing name: `Micodex – Watch Remote` (the bare name is unavailable in that locale); Chinese listing and in-app name: `Micodex`.
- Age questionnaire: the app has no content feed, web browser, social messaging, ads, health content, gambling, or mature content; Apple calculated 4+.
- Price: free, no subscriptions or in-app purchases.
- Support / marketing: https://kiskir.dev/projects/micodex
- Privacy: https://kiskir.dev/projects/micodex/privacy
- Terms: https://kiskir.dev/projects/micodex/terms
- Account sign-in: not required by Micodex. Codex use and its account are separate.
- Privacy label: no data collected by the developer; transient local audio and Codex's separate processing are explained in the policy.

Reviewers need a Mac with the receiver, a reachable local network, and BlackHole 2ch for real dictation. The Watch's More Options → Try Demo previews controls without operating a Mac, and is clearly labeled demo. A real test still requires the hardware and setup in the README.

Screenshots must be supplied by the app owner and must show the actual Watch interface. Keep the App Store version in Prepare for Submission until these assets are ready; do not claim public availability from upload alone.

Private review-contact data and upload receipts belong in ignored local artifacts, never in this public metadata directory.
